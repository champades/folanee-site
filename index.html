About FỌLÁNEE

Bold textiles.
Living colour.
Yoruba soul.

FỌLÁNEE is a fashion illustration and styling studio rooted in Yoruba heritage and African textile culture. Each illustration is a celebration of colour, craft and identity — fabric rendered as fine art.

From woven Aso-oke stripe gowns to sculptural corset silhouettes, every piece captures the soul of a garment — not just its silhouette.
